Privacy Policy

For the browser extension “Microcopy Lab” · Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short: Microcopy Lab has no server and no account. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything stays in your browser.

What the extension processes

  • Texts of websites you enable — visible texts, input placeholders, tooltips and accessibility labels. They are read in order to replace them with your own variants, and, if you leave automatic collecting switched on, stored as a list of “collected texts” so that you can build your table from it.
  • Your tables and settings — the contents of the spreadsheet you import, the variant you selected and the options you set per website.

Where the data is stored

All of it is stored locally in your browser profile using the browser's own extension storage (chrome.storage.local). There is no server, no user account, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. The extension never transmits this data anywhere.

Network access

The extension makes exactly one kind of network request: when you enter the address of a table — for example a Google Sheet shared by link, or a CSV file — it downloads that file so it can be used as your table. No cookies, page contents or other data from your browser are sent with that request. If you never enter such an address, the extension makes no network requests at all.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • storage — to keep your tables and settings in the browser.
  • scripting and website access — to replace texts on a website. Access is requested separately for each website, only after you click “Enable”, and you can withdraw it at any time in the browser's extension settings.
  • activeTab — to read the address of the current tab when you open the extension's popup, so that it can offer to enable itself for that website.

Deleting your data

Removing a website in the extension's settings deletes its table and its collected texts and withdraws the permission for that website. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its data.

Sharing and exports

Collected texts can contain personal data that was displayed on the pages you tested, for example names of people shown in a list. Nothing is exported unless you click “Export”. Please review an export before you pass it on.

Your rights

Because no data ever reaches us, we hold no personal data about you and there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete. The data lies in your own browser and is under your control at all times. If you have questions about this policy, write to the address above.

Changes to this policy

Changes will be published on this page and noted in the release notes of the extension. If the way data is handled ever changes materially, users will be informed before the change takes effect.
